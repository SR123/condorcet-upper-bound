1. Domains, slices, and the six-window inequality.

Let F_k be all nonempty full domains on k labels defined by one extreme
never-law xN1 or xN3 on each triple, together with arbitrary fixed pair
orientations. This larger class is necessary: conditional windows of a full
peak-pit domain may acquire pair constraints.
For H in F_6, write M(H)=|H| and let A(H) count all enabled adjacent-swap flags,
over its five adjacent positions and all orders. Group its orders by last
label to form bottom five-slices, and by first label to form top five-slices.
After deleting that endpoint, each nonempty slice is in F_5.

There are128400 labelled F_5 domains, in1160 relabelling classes. Put
  flow_c(H) = sum_(bottom slices of class c) |slice|
              - sum_(top slices of class c) |slice|.
The certificate specifies per-order class weights phi_c. Define
  s(H) = (12/19)M(H) + sum_c phi_c flow_c(H) - A(H)/5.
All s(H) are nonnegative, checked on the complete F_6 family below.

2. The exceptional types and the slack split.

Let R8 be the domain consisting of

It has a common first and last label. Removing its first label gives the

rt(H) as the NUMBER of top five-slices of class41. These are slice counts,
not counts of orders.

Let T be the five isomorphism classes identified in seven_seed_manifest.json:
sizes8,26,34,34,38. All are closed under reversal; s=0 and rb=rt on them,
and rb(R8)=rt(R8)=0. The two34-order classes have the same feature row.
Let S be the nine additional classes identified by the same manifest and
S_feature_rows in joint_8_3.certificate.json. These are specified classes,
not an assertion that every other class has positive slack.

Set eta=1/152. The exact certificate proves
  s(H) >= eta [ M(H)+4rb(H)+4rt(H) ],              H outside T union S. (F2)
On S it supplies nine rational values psi, one per specified feature class.
Define
  p(H)=psi(H) on S, and p(H)=2eta[rt(H)-rb(H)] otherwise;
  theta_b(H)=s(H)/2+p(H),   theta_t(H)=s(H)/2-p(H).
Outside T union S, F2 gives
  theta_b(H) >= eta M(H)/2 + 4eta rt(H),
  theta_t(H) >= eta M(H)/2 + 4eta rb(H).
On T both split values are zero. No positivity is required on S: bounded
endpoint errors suffice for the asymptotic argument and are removed below.

3. The seven-window inequality.

For J in F_7 let B_b(J),T_t(J) be its nonempty six-slices and set
  Lambda(J)=sum_b theta_b(B_b(J)) + sum_t theta_t(T_t(J)).
The certificate checks exactly that
  Lambda(J) >= eta |J|                                           (F3)
whenever J has a six-slice in T other than R8, or in S. To cover top slices,
both J and its reversal are checked and reversed seed types are included.
There are18 required seed families. Their complete enumeration gives14248
sets before deduplicating features, and18316 invariant feature rows after
including reversal. All18316 saved witnesses pass a separate tuple checker.

The remaining cases follow without enumeration. If all slices lie outside
T union S, the two displayed bounds sum to eta|J| because the bottom slices
partition J, as do the top slices. Otherwise the exceptional slices can
only be R8. Say there are a bottom and a' top R8 slices. Each bottom R8
slice has one top label, so its eight orders enter one top receiver slice.
That receiver has a bottom five-slice of class866. It cannot be R8, since
rb(R8)=0; it cannot be another T or S class because we are outside F3.
Distinct bottom R8 slices contribute distinct pairs (receiver,bottom label),
so the receiver terms 4eta rb provide at least4eta a. This exactly pays
eta*8/2 for each omitted eight-order R8 slice. Symmetrically, top R8 slices
receive4eta a'. R8 itself contributes zero. The total is at least
  eta(|J|-8a)/2 + 4eta a + eta(|J|-8a')/2 + 4eta a' = eta|J|.
Thus F3 holds for EVERY full seven-domain J in F_7.

5. Summation and removal of boundary errors.

In a full peak-pit-with-pairs domain D on n labels, condition on the entire
order outside each block of six consecutive positions. Its conditional
six-domain lies in F_6. Sum s(H) over these conditional domains. The class
potentials telescope: the last five-slice of one block is the first five-
slice of the next, with identical outside information. Each interior swap
flag occurs five times; only a bounded number of endpoint flag counts are
missing. Thus the total slack is
  (12/19)n|D| - A(D) + O(|D|),
where the implied constant is independent of n and D.

The identity theta_b+theta_t=s and the exact slice partitions express this
sum as the sum of Lambda over seven-blocks, up to the two endpoint split
terms. Because F_6 is a finite family and all specified weights are finite,
those terms are O(|D|), even if some theta on S are negative. The universal
seven-window bound therefore gives
  A(D) <= (12/19-1/152)n|D|+O(|D|) = (5/8)n|D|+O(|D|).
Insertion fibres of full peak-pit-with-pairs domains are intervals, so their
deletion sum B(D)=sum_x |D restricted off x| equals n|D|-A(D). Hence
  B(D) >= (3/8)n|D|-O(|D|).

Concatenate t copies of ANY fixed domain K on n labels on ordered disjoint
blocks. This remains a full peak-pit-with-pairs domain, has |K|^t orders,
and deletion sum t B(K)|K|^(t-1). Apply the preceding bound and divide by
t|K|^(t-1), then let t tend to infinity. The boundary disappears, giving
  B(K) >= (3/8)n|K|                                      for EVERY K.
For a largest peak-pit domain B<=n h(n-1), so h(n)<=(8/3)h(n-1).

6. General domains.

Use the component and gap lemmas from Moros's note, independently audited
in the preceding cycle. Every adjacent-swap component K of a full general
CD is a full peak-pit-with-pairs domain, and
  sum_K B(K)=Sigma(D)+G(D).
For a maximal general CD the gap theorem says
  G(D)<=3Sigma(D)-(n+1)|D|.
Combining with the3/8 deletion inequality yields
  4Sigma(D)>=[(11/8)n+1]|D|.
For a domain attaining f(n), Sigma(D)<=n f(n-1). Therefore
  f(n)<=[32n/(11n+8)] f(n-1) <= (32/11) f(n-1).
Starting with f(3)=h(3)=4 proves the displayed uniform bounds. Keeping the
factor n/(n+8/11) also yields f(n)=O(n^(-8/11)(32/11)^n).
