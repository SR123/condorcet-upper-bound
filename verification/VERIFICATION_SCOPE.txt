MAIN CONCLUSIONS AND SEMANTICS
All seven conclusions of the current Theorem 1.1 have end-to-end Lean proofs
for the actual maximum Condorcet-domain functions. Entry points are in
lean/CDCert/Main.lean; see docs/PAPER_LEAN_MAP.txt for the revised numbering.
The first four results assume n >=3; the two root-limit results and polynomial
correction have no additional hypotheses. Orders are strict total orders;
Condorcet requires transitive majority for every odd profile, with repetitions.
The proved value-restriction equivalence matches the current combinatorial
definition. Peak-pit uses extreme triple conditions without a common axis.

The component reduction/counts, free-to-locked injection, maximal-domain gap
inequality, fixed-pair finite coverage, both endpoint orientations, R8 receiver,
window conditioning/cancellation, ordered copies, base case, recurrences,
limits and polynomial correction are all in the checked dependency chain.
The conditional FreeLockedInjection helper is discharged before Main.
The local inequality is CDCert.K6.finite_lemma, with denominator scaling
cost10=10*C and qD=2*d*q. Finite certificates and their coverage are checked
within Lean's kernel. Python/C++ execution is not a formal premise.
