THEOREM 1.1 -- ALL SEVEN CONCLUSIONS
All seven entry points are in lean/CDCert/Main.lean.

CDCert.Main.general_recurrence
$n\geq3$: $(11n+8)f(n)\leq32nf(n-1)$.
exact natural-number statement

CDCert.Main.general_geometric
$n\geq3$: $f(n)\leq4(32/11)^{n-3}$.
counts cast to real numbers

CDCert.Main.peak_recurrence
$n\geq3$: $3h(n)\leq8h(n-1)$.
exact natural-number statement

CDCert.Main.peak_geometric
$n\geq3$: $h(n)\leq4(8/3)^{n-3}$.
counts cast to real numbers

CDCert.Main.general_limit
No extra premises: $\exists L_f$, $f(n)^{1/n}\to L_f\leq32/11$.
real powers; convergence at infinity

CDCert.Main.peak_limit
No extra premises: $\exists L_h$, $h(n)^{1/n}\to L_h\leq8/3$.
real powers; convergence at infinity

CDCert.Main.general_polynomial_correction
No extra premises: $f(n)=O(n^{-8/11}(32/11)^n)$.
real powers and big-O at infinity

SEMANTIC SCOPE

transitivity of majority for every odd profile, allowing repetitions.
The proved value-restriction equivalence identifies this with the paper's
combinatorial Definition 2.1. Peak-pit imposes an extreme condition on every
triple and does not assume a common axis. The final f/h are actual maxima.
